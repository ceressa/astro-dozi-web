Dream interpretation is the art of decoding subconscious messages in symbols seen during sleep. Astro Dozi analyzes your dream, catalogs its symbols, reads emotional undercurrents, and reveals possible meaning in your waking life.

📚 History of Dream Interpretation

Dream interpretation spans both religious and psychological traditions. Starting with ancient Egypt's Chester Beatty III "Dream Book," developing through Ibn Sirin's Islamic interpretations, entering modern psychology with Freud and Jung - this practice seeks the root meaning of symbols in the subconscious.

🔎 How to Do Dream Interpretation

  • Upon waking, jot down your dream; details fade fast
  • Write dream's core elements: who, what, where, feeling
  • Select the dominant emotion (happy, scared, strange)
  • Let AI extract symbols and blend them with your context
  • Compare suggested meaning to real-life situations

💤 Common Symbols in Dream Interpretation

  • Water: Emotional state, subconscious, cleansing or drowning
  • Flying: Desire for freedom, loss of control, opening to dreams
  • Losing Teeth: Transformation anxiety, loss of control, life transition
  • Snake: Transformation, threat, healing power, hidden knowledge
  • House: Your self, spiritual space, state of your identity
  • Baby: New project, beginning, a quality needing protection

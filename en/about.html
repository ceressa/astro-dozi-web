Astro Dozi is a personal astrology app that combines real astronomical calculations from your birth chart with artificial intelligence. We don't produce templated zodiac readings: we generate fresh content every time based on the combination of your sun, moon, and rising sign.

✨ Why Astro Dozi?

Most astrology apps on the App Store fall into two categories: either they serve static pre-written zodiac readings, or they send generic commands to a ChatGPT-style API and claim to be "AI."

Astro Dozi is different: it calculates planetary positions in real-time using Swiss Ephemeris on your device. AI interprets this verified astronomical data - never inventing. Additionally, we offer features like coffee ground reading and palm reading that analyze real objects (cup, hand) via computer vision - a first on the App Store.

🧮 Our Technology Stack

  • Swiss Ephemeris: Planetary position calculation with NASA JPL data, same engine as planetarium software
  • Google Gemini AI: Multimodal interpretation (text, vision, audio)
  • Google Imagen: AI generation of soulmate portraits
  • Firebase: Real-time data, secure authentication
  • Flutter + Native iOS: WidgetKit, Live Activities, Siri Shortcuts

🌟 Our Philosophy

Astrology is not science: it's entertainment and a tool for personal reflection. All content in Astro Dozi is presented within this frame: we don't promise miracle cures, financial advice, or medical counsel. We don't determine fate. We're a dance partner with the stars: you make decisions, we offer symbols.

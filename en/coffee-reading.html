📚 History of Coffee Reading

Coffee ground reading has deep roots in Ottoman palace tradition. With coffee's arrival in Anatolia in the 16th century, reading grounds became part of daily life. Symbol families - birds, roads, letters, hearts - passed through generations. Today it remains one of Turkey's most beloved fortune practices.

🔎 How to Do Coffee Reading

  • Before drinking Turkish coffee, clarify your intention and think of loved ones
  • After finishing, cover the cup and flip it upside down
  • Wait 5-10 minutes for grounds to settle
  • Photograph the cup's interior from 4 different angles
  • AI identifies each symbol and interprets it by your sign and wish

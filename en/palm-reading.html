Palm reading (hand reading, palmistry) is the art of extracting insight about personality and life from the shape and length of lines in your hand. Astro Dozi brings digital depth to this ancient art through AI vision analyzing your hand's photo.

📚 History of Palm Reading

Palm reading traces back 4,000 years to India's Vedic tradition. Studied by Aristotle in ancient Greece, merged with traditional medicine in China. In the West, 19th-century science called it "chiromancy." Three main lines (heart, life, head) and the active hand (your writing hand) hold central importance.

🔎 How to Do Palm Reading

  • Choose your active hand (usually your writing hand)
  • Face your palm in well-lit space
  • Gently stretch your hand so lines are clear
  • Photograph your palm with all lines fully visible
  • Let AI separately interpret heart - life - head - fate lines

🧠 Astro Dozi's Method

Astro Dozi's palm analysis uses Gemini's vision model to detect main lines (heart, life, head, fate) from your hand photo. Each line's length, depth, and starting point match traditional chiromancy data. Result personalizes by birth chart. Like ground reading, this is computer vision-based - nowhere else on App Store.

✋ Common Symbols in Palm Reading

  • Life Line: Life energy, health, major life changes
  • Heart Line: Romantic relationships, emotional depth, love life
  • Head Line: Mental ability, decision style, thinking structure
  • Fate Line: Career, success, external influence
  • Sun Line: Artistic talent, recognition, path to success
  • Marriage Line: Significant relationships, marriage or deep bonds

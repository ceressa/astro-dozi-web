Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers, clear information

⚙ General

Is Astro Dozi free?

Yes, basic features are free. Daily zodiac reading, birth chart, fortune wheel - no subscription required. Premium tiers (Gold, Diamond, Platinum) unlock additional features and unlimited fortune readings.

What platforms does it run on?

Currently live on Google Play Store for Android phones and tablets. iOS is coming - join the waitlist to be notified. You can also use the web version in your browser.

What languages does it support?

Currently Turkish only. All interpretations, AI chat, and UI were custom-built for Turkish users. English web pages are available at astro.dozi.app/en/

💵 Subscription & Payment

How do I cancel my premium?

Cancel through Google Play Store: Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Astro Dozi, Cancel subscription. We can't handle cancellation from the app because payment is managed by Google.

What are the subscription tiers?

Gold: ₵179.99/month. Diamond: ₵349.99/month. Platinum: ₵599.99/month. Annual subscriptions save 33%.

Can I get a refund?

Google Play allows automatic refunds within 48 hours of purchase. After that, you can request a refund through Google.

✨ Stardust and Fortunes

What is Stardust?

Stardust is the in-app virtual currency. Spend it on fortune readings. Free users get 25 Stardust as a welcome bonus.

How do I earn Stardust?

Daily login bonus (Free: 3 Stardust, Gold: 10, Diamond: 20, Platinum: 30), watch rewarded ads (+5), complete daily tasks, streak bonuses (7d=5, 14d=10, 21d=15, 28d+=20), and invite friends (25-50 Stardust).

How long does a fortune reading take?

Usually 10-30 seconds. AI combines your birth chart, past interactions, and current transits to generate your reading.

🔒 Privacy & Data

Is my information safe?

All personal data is encrypted and stored in Google Firebase. Only you can access your own data. Your birth date, birth time, and photos are never shared with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings, Account, Delete Account. All your personal data (profile, fortune history, dream journal) is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Are you GDPR compliant?

Yes. See our GDPR Notice and full Privacy Policy.

🧠 AI & Interpretation

How accurate are the AI readings?

Astro Dozi's AI is based on real astronomical data from your birth chart (via Swiss Ephemeris). The interpretation is AI-generated and for entertainment. We don't promise miracle cures, financial gains, or definite future predictions.

Which AI model do you use?

Google Gemini multimodal model. Used for text interpretation, vision (photo analysis), and image generation (soulmate portraits via Imagen).

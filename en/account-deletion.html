Account Deletion

Last Updated: 5 March 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Astro Dozi account and all associated personal data. Account deletion is irreversible.

1. What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following data is permanently removed:

  • Profile Information: Your name, email address, profile photo, birth date/time, and birth place
  • Zodiac Data: Your zodiac sign, rising sign calculations, and birth chart data
  • Stardust Balance: Your entire Stardust balance and transaction history
  • Daily Readings: Past daily zodiac reading data and cache
  • Tarot History: Your tarot reading history and results
  • Compatibility Results: Your zodiac compatibility results
  • Entertainment Features: Aura reading, past life analysis, chakra analysis, and life path results
  • Usage Data: App usage history, login streak data, and activity logs
  • Membership Information: Your subscription tier and related data
  • Firebase Authentication: The authentication record linked to your Google account

2. Delete from within the App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly from the app. The process is instant:

  • Step 1: Open the Astro Dozi app and sign in
  • Step 2: Go to the Profile tab in the bottom menu
  • Step 3: Tap Account Management
  • Step 4: Tap "Delete My Account"
  • Step 5: Select "Yes, Delete" in the confirmation dialog

Your account and all data are deleted instantly and permanently.

3. Request Deletion by Email

If you do not have access to the app or cannot complete deletion from within it, follow these steps:

  • Send an email from the Google account email address you used to register
  • Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  • Send the email to astrodozi@dozi.app

Email deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. A confirmation email will be sent once complete.

4. Subscription Warning

If you have an active premium subscription, please complete the following steps before deleting your account:

  • Android: Google Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Astro Dozi → Cancel
  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Astro Dozi → Cancel Subscription

Important: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. If you do not cancel separately, billing may continue.

5. Data Retained After Deletion

After your account is deleted, some data may be retained for a limited period due to legal obligations:

  • Payment Records: Payment transaction records are retained for up to 10 years as required by tax and accounting regulations
  • Error Reports: Anonymous crash reports may be retained to improve the app (contains no personal information)

This data is not used to personally identify you.

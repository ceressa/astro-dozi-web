Account Deletion

Last Updated: March 5, 2026

This page explains how you can permanently delete your Astro Dozi account and all of your personal data. Account deletion is irreversible.

1. Data to Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following data is permanently removed:

  • Profile Information: Your name, e-mail address, profile photo, birth date/time and birth place
  • Zodiac Data: Your zodiac sign selection, rising sign calculations and birth chart information
  • Stardust Balance: Your entire Stardust balance and transaction history
  • Daily Horoscope Readings: Past daily horoscope reading data and cache
  • Tarot Reading: Your tarot reading history and results
  • Zodiac Compatibility: Your zodiac compatibility results
  • Fun Features: Your aura reading, past life analysis, chakra analysis and life path results
  • Usage Data: Your app usage history, login streak data and activity records
  • Membership Information: Your membership tier and related data
  • Firebase Authentication: Your authentication record associated with your Google account

2. Deletion From Within the App (Recommended)

You can delete your account most quickly directly from within the app. The process takes effect instantly:

  • Step 1: Open the Astro Dozi app and sign in
  • Step 2: Go to the Profile tab from the bottom menu
  • Step 3: Tap the Account Management option
  • Step 4: Tap the "Delete My Account" button
  • Step 5: Select "Yes, Delete" in the confirmation dialog

Your account and all of your data are deleted instantly and permanently.

3. Deletion Request by E-mail

If you do not have access to the app or cannot perform the deletion from within the app, follow the steps below:

  • Send an e-mail from the e-mail address of the Google account you registered with
  • Write "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line
  • Send the e-mail to astrodozi@dozi.app

Deletion requests made by e-mail are processed within 7 business days at the latest. A confirmation e-mail is sent when the process is complete.

4. Subscription Notice

If you have an active premium subscription, please follow the steps below before deleting your account:

  • Android: Google Play Store > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > Astro Dozi > Cancel
  • iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Astro Dozi > Cancel Subscription

Important: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your active subscription. If you do not cancel your subscription separately, charges may continue.

5. Retained Data

After your account is deleted, some data may be retained for a certain period under legal obligations:

  • Payment Records: Payment transaction records are retained for 10 years as required by tax and accounting obligations
  • Error Reports: Anonymous error reports may be retained for the purpose of improving the app (they do not contain personal information)

This data is not used to personally identify you.

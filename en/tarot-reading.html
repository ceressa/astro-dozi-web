Tarot reading is an intuitive practice where cards drawn from a 78-card deck offer insight into past, present, and future through symbolism. Astro Dozi merges the classic Rider-Waite-Smith deck with AI interpretation power; each draw gains personal meaning.

📚 History of Tarot Reading

Tarot began in 15th-century Italy as a card game, becoming mystical practice in the 18th century. Today it ranks among the world's most widespread divination systems. The 22 Major Arcana represent life's core archetypes (Fool, Magician, Empress, Lovers); 56 Minor Arcana reflect daily life details.

🔎 How to Do Tarot Reading

  • Clarify your intention: love, career, general guidance
  • Ask your question aloud, open your heart
  • Shuffle the deck intuitively and draw three cards
  • Choose a spread: Past - Present - Future or Celtic Cross
  • AI combines card symbolism with your birth chart for interpretation

🧠 Astro Dozi's Method

Astro Dozi offers the full 78-card RWS deck. Single card, 3-card (past-present-future), or Celtic Cross spreads are possible. For each card, Gemini AI merges the card's meaning with your zodiac sign, birth chart, and question context. Unlike static tarot apps, the same card produces different readings on different days.

🃏 Common Symbols in Tarot Reading

  • Fool (0): New beginning, bold step, limitless potential
  • Lovers (VI): Romantic love, choice, step toward partnership
  • Wheel (X): Cycles change, fortune, fate's turn
  • Star (XVII): Hope, renewal, spiritual guidance
  • Moon (XVIII): Illusion, intuition, mysterious insights
  • World (XXI): Completion, success, cycle's end

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3, 2026 · Effective: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, for which purposes it is processed, with whom it is shared, how long it is retained and which rights you have, through the Astro Dozi mobile application, web application and the astro.dozi.app website (collectively, the "Service") provided by Bardino Technology ("Company", "we", "data controller"). This Policy has been prepared in accordance with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the United Kingdom UK GDPR, the United States COPPA and CCPA/CPRA, as well as the Google Play and Apple App Store developer policies.

1. Identity of the Data Controller

  • Company: Bardino Technology
  • Application: Astro Dozi (package name: com.bardino.zodi)
  • Website: astro.dozi.app
  • Contact e-mail: astrodozi@dozi.app

For requests under the KVKK, this address applies as the Data Controller, and under the GDPR/UK GDPR as the Controller. A separate representative has been appointed within the EU and data subjects can reach them at the same e-mail address.

2. Summary at a Glance

  • To create your account, you sign in with Google or Apple; your name, e-mail and profile picture are obtained.
  • For personalised astrology content, your birth date, birth time and birth place (optional) are processed.
  • Readings are generated through Google Gemini AI using anonymised prompts; your raw birth data is not sent to the AI provider for storage.
  • Payments are made through Google Play and the Apple App Store, and subscription status is synced via RevenueCat. We do not see or store your card details.
  • Ads are shown only in the free version via Google AdMob; on iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission is requested.
  • Our data storage location is Google Cloud / Firebase (EU and US regions). International transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • You can delete your account, download your data or withdraw your consent at any time from within the app.

3. Personal Data Processed and Their Categories

3.1 Identity and Contact Data

  • Sign in with Google: Your first name, last name, Google e-mail address, profile photo, Google user identifier (UID).
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple user identifier, name and e-mail (the relay address if you use Apple's private-relay e-mail).
  • Firebase authentication: Internal anonymous user identifier (Firebase UID), session tokens.

3.2 Astrology and Profile Data

  • Zodiac sign selection and zodiac preferences
  • Birth date (day, month, year), optional
  • Birth time, for rising sign and birth chart (optional)
  • Birth place (city/coordinates), only for rising sign and chart calculation (optional)
  • Gender, interests, personalisation preferences (optional)
  • Profile avatar / theme selection

3.3 In-App Activity Data

  • Stardust balance and transaction history (earning/spending)
  • Daily login streak, daily bonus records
  • Tarot readings, zodiac compatibility results, aura/chakra/past life/life path results
  • AI chat (chatbot) conversation history
  • Notification preferences and read status
  • Membership tier (Standard / Gold / Diamond / Platinum)

3.4 Device, Technical and Diagnostic Data

  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, language and region setting
  • Application version, build number, installation time
  • IP address (in truncated form), approximate location (country level)
  • Advertising identifier (GAID/IDFA, only in the free version and if you give permission)
  • Crash reports, error traces, performance metrics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Install source (Google Play Install Referrer, for campaign attribution)

3.5 Device Sensors Processed With Permission

The following accesses work only when you use the relevant feature and after you have given permission; the data is not sent to our servers:

  • Camera / gallery: Profile photo, photo for aura reading, QR code scanning
  • Microphone: Voice command / speech-to-text feature
  • Accelerometer/sensor: Interactive features such as drawing a daily card by "shaking"
  • Notifications: Push for daily horoscope, reminders and important updates

3.6 Payment and Subscription Data

All purchases take place through Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS). Subscription status, proof of purchase and renewal information are synced via RevenueCat, Inc. The Company does not see or store financial data such as your credit card number, expiry date, CVC or bank account information. The payment data we hold consists only of the anonymous product identifier, subscription status, purchase time and in-app receipt/orderId records.

3.7 Data Relating to Children

The Service is intended for users aged 13 and over (16 and over in the EU/EEA). Data is not knowingly collected from individuals below this age. If this is noticed, the relevant data is deleted immediately.

4. Purposes of Processing, Legal Bases and Data

Our legal bases under KVKK Art. 5/Art. 6 and GDPR Articles 6/9 are summarised in the table below:

4.1 Establishment and Performance of the Contract

  • Purpose: Creating your account, providing the Service, subscription management.
  • Data: Identity, profile, subscription, in-app activity.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5/2-c (performance of the contract); GDPR Art. 6(1)(b).

4.2 Personalisation Based on Explicit Consent

  • Purpose: Rising sign, birth chart, personalised AI readings, tarot and aura analyses.
  • Data: Birth date/time/place, inputs received via camera/microphone.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5/1 (explicit consent); GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) for sensitive preferences that could constitute special-category data.

4.3 Legitimate Interest

  • Purpose: Error correction, fraud prevention, product development, security, anti-cheat.
  • Data: Device/technical data, crash reports, anonymous usage statistics.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5/2-f; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), a balancing test has been carried out.

4.4 Legal Obligation

  • Purpose: Tax, accounting, consumer law obligations, requests from competent authorities.
  • Data: Invoice/subscription records, contact data where necessary.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5/2-ç; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c).

4.5 Marketing (only with consent)

  • Purpose: Personalised advertising (AdMob), e-mail campaigns, push promotions.
  • Data: Advertising identifier, interest inferences, push token.
  • Legal basis: Explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Automated Decision-Making and Artificial Intelligence

Astrology content is generated using Google's Gemini large language model. A prompt containing your birth date, zodiac sign and free-text questions is created and sent to the AI; your raw profile data is not stored at the AI provider. The generated readings are intended solely for entertainment and personal development; they should not be used for health, financial or legal decisions. We do not operate any fully automated decision mechanism that affects you in a legal or similarly significant manner; there is no profile-based automated decision-making within the meaning of GDPR Art. 22.

6.2 Artificial Intelligence

  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC): Generation of horoscope, tarot, chat and other AI content. Terms · Data usage.

7. International Data Transfer

Your data may be processed on the Google Cloud infrastructure predominantly in the europe-west and us regions. Transfers made outside the EU/EEA, Turkey or the United Kingdom are subject to the following safeguards:

  • Direct transfer to countries with an adequacy decision from the European Commission,
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) and the necessary additional measures (encryption, anonymisation, audit rights),
  • The International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) for the UK GDPR,
  • Under the KVKK, explicit consent or the authorisation of the Personal Data Protection Board is obtained for countries that do not provide adequate protection.

8. Data Retention Periods

  • Active account data: As long as the account is active.
  • Inactive account: Deleted after a notice when no login has occurred for 24 months.
  • Account deletion request: Removed from all production systems within 30 days at the latest, and from backups within 90 days at the latest.
  • Crash/error logs: 90 days.
  • Analytics records: Automatically anonymised after 14 months.
  • Payment/invoice records: 10 years as required by tax legislation.
  • Legal dispute records: For the duration of the limitation period.

9. Data Security

  • All client-server traffic is encrypted with TLS 1.2+; HSTS is enforced.
  • Row-level access control via Firestore Security Rules; a user can only access their own documents.
  • All authorised operations invoked through Cloud Functions are verified with a Firebase Auth token.
  • API keys are stored on the server side; only scope-restricted public keys are present on the client.
  • Continuous monitoring for unusual access and abnormal spending patterns.
  • Regular dependency updates, static analysis, security reviews.
  • Staff access is based on need (least privilege) and is logged.

10. Your User Rights

Under the applicable legislation (KVKK Art. 11, GDPR Art. 15-22, CCPA/CPRA) you have the following rights:

  • Information and access: Learning whether your personal data is processed, its categories and recipients.
  • Rectification: Requesting correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Erasure / right to be forgotten: Requesting deletion of your data, except for legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction of processing: Requesting that processing be stopped in certain cases.
  • Data portability: Receiving your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Objecting to processing based on legitimate interest or to direct marketing.
  • Withdrawal of explicit consent: Requesting that processing initiated with consent be stopped.
  • Objection to automated decisions: Opposing automated decisions producing significant effects (no such decision-making takes place in the app).
  • Complaint: Lodging a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Authority (Turkey) or the competent data protection authority of an EU member state.
  • CCPA "Do Not Sell or Share": California residents may object to the sale/sharing of their personal information; we do not sell data, but you can use the channel below to opt out of sharing for advertising purposes.

Send your request to astrodozi@dozi.app; we respond within 30 days at the latest. For identity verification, you must contact us from your registered e-mail.

11. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account from within the app or by e-mail. See the Account Deletion page for detailed steps. If you have an active subscription, you must also cancel it via Google Play / Apple App Store; deleting your account does not automatically cancel the subscription.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

The mobile application does not use classic browser cookies; preference and cache information is stored on the device with shared_preferences. On the website, only essential functional localStorage is used; no tracking cookies are used. Advertising and measurement work only within the app, if the relevant permissions have been granted.

13. Data Breach Notification

  • In the event of a high-risk breach, affected users are informed without undue delay, within 72 hours at the latest, via in-app notification and/or e-mail.
  • Notification is made to the Personal Data Protection Authority and the relevant EU supervisory authority within the legal time limits.
  • A root-cause analysis is carried out after the incident and remedial measures are taken.

14. Changes to the Policy

This policy may be revised due to legislative changes or service updates. In the case of significant changes, you are notified via in-app notification and the "Last Updated" date; where necessary, explicit consent is requested again.

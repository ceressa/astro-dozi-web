Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 3, 2026 - Effective: May 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bardino Technology ("Company", "we", "data controller") collects, processes, shares, and retains your personal data through the Astro Dozi mobile app, web app, and astro.dozi.app website (collectively "Service"). This Policy complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK UK GDPR, US COPPA and CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play and Apple App Store developer policies.

1. Data Controller

  • Company: Bardino Technology
  • App: Astro Dozi (package name: com.bardino.zodi)
  • Website: astro.dozi.app
  • Contact email: astrodozi@dozi.app

2. Quick Summary

  • You sign in with Google or Apple; we collect your name, email, and profile picture.
  • For personalized astrology, we process your birth date, time, and location (optional).
  • Interpretations are generated via Google Gemini AI using anonymized prompts; raw birth data is not stored with the AI provider.
  • Payments go through Google Play and Apple App Store; subscription status syncs via RevenueCat. We don't see or store card details.
  • Ads appear only in the free version via Google AdMob; iOS includes App Tracking Transparency.
  • Data is stored in Google Cloud - Firebase (EU and US regions). International transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • You can delete your account, download your data, or withdraw consent anytime from the app.

3. Personal Data Categories

3.1 Identity & Contact Data

  • Google Sign-In: Name, surname, Google email, profile photo, Google user ID.
  • Apple Sign-In: Apple user ID, name and email (or Apple's private-relay email).
  • Firebase Auth: Internal anonymous user ID, session tokens.

3.2 Astrology & Profile Data

  • Birth date (day, month, year) - optional
  • Birth time - for rising sign and birth chart - optional
  • Birth location (city/coordinates) - for rising sign and chart only - optional
  • Gender, interests, customization preferences - optional
  • Profile avatar / theme choice

3.3 In-App Activity Data

  • Stardust balance and transaction history
  • Daily login streak, bonus records
  • Tarot readings, zodiac compatibility results
  • AI chatbot conversation history
  • Notification preferences and read status
  • Subscription tier (Free - Gold - Diamond - Platinum)

3.4 Device, Technical & Diagnostics Data

  • Device model, manufacturer, OS version, language, region
  • App version, build number, install time
  • IP address (anonymized), approximate location (country level)
  • Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA - free version only, with permission)
  • Crash reports, error traces, performance metrics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

3.5 Device Sensors (Permission-Based)

The following only work when you use the feature and grant permission; data is NOT sent to our servers:

  • Camera - gallery: Profile photo, aura reading photo, QR scanning
  • Microphone: Voice commands - speech-to-text feature
  • Accelerometer: Interactive features like "shake to draw"
  • Notifications: Daily horoscope, reminders, important updates

3.6 Payment & Subscription Data

All purchases go through Google Play Billing (Android) and Apple App Store - StoreKit (iOS). Subscription status and purchase records sync via RevenueCat, Inc. We do NOT see or store credit card numbers, expiration dates, CVCs, or bank account information. We only keep anonymous product IDs, subscription status, purchase time, and app receipt records.

3.7 Children's Data

The Service is for ages 13+ (16+ in EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If discovered, such data is deleted immediately.

4. Processing Purposes & Legal Basis

4.1 Contract Performance

  • Purpose: Create your account, deliver Service, manage subscription.
  • Data: Identity, profile, subscription, in-app activity.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (contract performance).

4.2 Consent-Based Personalization

  • Purpose: Rising sign, birth chart, personalized AI readings, tarot analysis.
  • Data: Birth date/time/location, camera/microphone inputs.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) (explicit consent).

4.3 Legitimate Interest

  • Purpose: Bug fixes, fraud prevention, product improvement, security, anti-cheat.
  • Data: Device/technical data, crash reports, anonymous usage stats.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest balancing test applied).

4.4 Legal Obligation

  • Purpose: Tax, accounting, consumer protection, government requests.
  • Data: Invoice/subscription records, contact info as needed.
  • Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) (legal obligation).

4.5 Marketing (Consent Only)

  • Purpose: Personalized ads (AdMob), email campaigns, push promotions.
  • Data: Advertising ID, inferred interests, push token.
  • Legal basis: Explicit consent - withdraw anytime.

5. AI & Automated Decision-Making

Astrology content is generated via Google's Gemini model. We create a prompt with your birth date, sign, and questions, then send it to AI. Raw profile data is not stored at the AI provider. Readings are for entertainment and personal reflection only - not medical, financial, or legal advice. We do not employ fully automated decision-making that has legal or similarly significant effect on you; GDPR Art. 22 restrictions do not apply.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Recipients

6.1 Infrastructure & Auth

  • Google Firebase - Google Cloud: Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage, Hosting.
  • Apple, Inc.: Sign in with Apple, push services, App Store purchases.

6.2 AI

  • Google Gemini API: Zodiac, tarot, chat, and other AI content generation.

6.3 Analytics & Diagnostics

  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash and error reports.

6.4 Push & Notifications

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android): Push delivery.
  • Apple Push Notification Service (iOS): Push delivery.

6.5 Ads (Free Version Only)

  • Google AdMob: Ad display and rewarded ads.
  • AdMob mediation partners: AppLovin and Unity Technologies for ad serving.

On iOS, your IDFA is not shared without App Tracking Transparency permission. On Android, you can reset your Advertising ID or disable personalized ads in device settings.

6.6 Payment & Subscription

  • Google Play Billing: Android purchases.
  • Apple App Store - StoreKit: iOS purchases.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Subscription status sync, purchase validation.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed in Google Cloud europe-west and us regions. Transfers outside EU/EEA are protected by:

  • EU Commission adequacy decisions for qualifying countries
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) with supplementary measures (encryption, anonymization)
  • UK GDPR via International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)

8. Data Retention Periods

  • Active account data: While account is active.
  • Inactive account: Deleted after 24 months with warning.
  • Deletion request: Removed from production within 30 days, from backups within 90 days.
  • Crash/error logs: 90 days.
  • Analytics: Anonymized after 14 months.
  • Payment/invoice records: 10 years (tax requirement).
  • Legal dispute records: Duration of statute of limitations.

9. Data Security

  • All traffic encrypted with TLS 1.2+; HSTS applied.
  • Firestore Security Rules enforce row-level access control.
  • API calls authenticated with Firebase Auth tokens.
  • API keys stored server-side; client uses only public/scoped keys.
  • Continuous monitoring for unusual access and spending patterns.
  • Regular dependency updates, static analysis, security reviews.
  • Personnel access on least-privilege principle, fully logged.

10. Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access & information: Learn what data we process and why.
  • Correction: Fix inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure - right to be forgotten: Request deletion (except for legal retention obligations).
  • Restrict processing: Suspend processing in certain cases.
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Object: Opt-out of legitimate-interest or direct-marketing processing.
  • Withdraw consent: Stop consent-based processing anytime.
  • File a complaint: With your national data protection authority.

Submit requests to astrodozi@dozi.app. We'll respond within 30 days. Use your registered email for identity verification.

11. Account & Data Deletion

Delete your account in-app. Note: deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription - cancel that separately via Google Play / Apple App Store.

12. Cookies & Local Storage

The mobile app uses shared_preferences for preferences and cache - not browser cookies. The website uses minimal localStorage for basic functionality. No tracking cookies. Ads and analytics only in the app with relevant permissions.

13. Data Breach Notification

  • High-risk breaches: affected users notified within 72 hours via in-app notification and/or email.
  • Regulatory authorities notified per legal timelines.
  • Root-cause analysis and remedial measures applied.

14. Policy Changes

This policy may be revised for regulatory changes or service updates. Significant changes will be announced in-app with an updated "Last Updated" date. Explicit reconsent required where necessary.

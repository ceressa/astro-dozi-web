A birth chart (natal chart) is a map of where the planets were in the sky at the moment you were born. It goes beyond your sun sign: combined with your moon sign, rising sign, planetary placements, and houses, it reveals your complete astrological identity. No two people have the same birth chart.

☀ Key Elements in Your Birth Chart

  • Sun Sign: Your inner self, the core of your identity, the answer to "who am I"
  • Moon Sign: Your emotional world, subconscious, inner need for security
  • Rising Sign (AC): Your outward face, first impressions, approach to life
  • Mercury Sign: Your communication style, way of thinking, learning method
  • Venus Sign: Your understanding of love, romantic preferences, aesthetic world
  • Mars Sign: Your action style, anger management, source of motivation
  • 12 Houses: Planetary influences across 12 different areas of life

🧮 How Is a Birth Chart Calculated?

Three pieces of information are critical for an accurate birth chart:

  • Birth date: Day, month, year (Gregorian calendar)
  • Birth time: As precise as possible (to the minute). A 15-minute difference can change the rising sign.
  • Birth place: City, or even exact address. Two people born at the same time in different cities can have different rising signs.

Astro Dozi sends this information to Swiss Ephemeris, an academic astronomical engine that uses NASA JPL planetary position data and delivers results with the same accuracy as planetarium software.

🏠 The Meanings of the 12 Houses

  • 1st House (Rising): Self, body, first impressions
  • 2nd House: Money, possessions, values
  • 3rd House: Communication, siblings, immediate environment
  • 4th House: Family, home, roots, mother figure
  • 5th House: Creativity, romance, children
  • 6th House: Health, daily routine, work life
  • 7th House: Marriage, partnerships, relationships
  • 8th House: Transformation, shared resources, secrets
  • 9th House: Philosophy, travel, higher education
  • 10th House: Career, status, father figure
  • 11th House: Friends, dreams, communities
  • 12th House: Subconscious, intuition, isolation
